## Steps

### Step 1 — recap data on the server

No secret goes into the recap: it reads the public events (seed removed, `publicEvent`), names come from the live view's name map, and it lists no card and no undrawn number. A round end and a cut already make every screen fetch its state (they are not appendable frames), so the recap needs no new socket message; host, players, eliminated players and spectators fetch the same state.

## FOR THE OWNER

**How to see the recap.** Sign in at http://localhost:5390 with Dev sign-in and choose Host a match:

1. Pick **Royale**.
2. Under **Between rounds**, choose **Wait a few seconds** with a number of seconds, or **I start each round myself**. With the default 6 s you see only the first card, so pick 25 s or "I start each round myself" to see all of them.
3. Create the match, press **Fill with bots** in its lobby, then start it.
4. Draw through the first round. When it ends, the recap appears: under Start next round on the host console, and under the round's result on every player's screen, including players who are out and spectators.

The Quick Test (menu: Quick Test) shows the same recap at each royale round end.

**What each card shows.** Only cards that have something to show appear. With a timed pause each card stays at least 5 s; when time runs short the least important cards are dropped. With "I start each round myself" the cards go round until you start the round. Previous and Next step through them by hand.

1. **Top of the round:** the three players who scored the most points in this round, on a podium.
2. **Biggest climber:** the player who moved up the most places, "from #7 to #3".
3. **The cut:** how many players are out and their names (up to 8, then "and N more"), and who a shield saved.
4. **Seasons and speed:** who collected all four seasons in this round, and the fastest tapper (own taps only, at least 5).
5. **Round facts:** how many numbers were drawn and how many players are left.

The recap changes nothing: no points, no shields, no ranking.

**Suggested pause length:** 25 s between royale rounds, enough for all five cards. The default stays 6 s until you decide (see NEEDS).
