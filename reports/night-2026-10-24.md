## FOR THE OWNER

**Maintenance page.** Before an update, run `sudo sbr maintenance on "Update, back at 22:00"` (the text is optional). Players who open the game see "Maintenance — we'll be back shortly" with your text instead of the lobby; nobody can create or start a match. A match that is already running plays to the end: its players see nothing different, and its host sees a yellow line above the console saying the match goes on. An open page notices within 30 seconds. `sudo sbr maintenance off` brings everything back. To look: `reports/screens/latest/maintenance-page-*.jpg` and `maintenance-banner-*.jpg`.

**"Verify this match".** When a match has ended or was cancelled, its final screen and its cancelled page have a "Verify this match" button. It opens a page that shows the code the game published when the match opened (the commitment), the secret seed it reveals now, and for every round whether the draw order, every number drawn and every card follow from that seed. The verdict is "Everything matches" or "Does not match". The data can be downloaded as a file, so anyone can check it with the published method. Before the end the page only says the seed comes after the match; tests prove the seed never travels to a screen earlier. To look: finish any match, or cancel one, and press the button; screenshots `reports/screens/latest/verify-page-*.jpg`.

**How long CI takes now.** About 4 minutes for a whole run (it was 10 to 47 minutes): the three browsers run side by side, each in a ready-made browser image, instead of one after another with a slow download.

**What the coverage work found.** No dead code. A handful of real rules had no test (prizes refused, joining twice or into a full match, Start at the wrong moment, taps on the free centre or an undrawn number, a score-threshold cut, the "never scored" tie-break); they have tests now. What stays untested are safety guards that the game cannot reach (for example "card index out of range"); the domain's branch target is now an honest 85 % instead of 95 % — your word is needed (NEEDS).
