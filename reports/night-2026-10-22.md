- **Too few players at a set start time**: fewer than 2 → the match is stopped ("Not enough players at the start time"); 2 or more → it starts with whoever is there (the spec's minPlayers of 4 is not enforced; the spec says nothing about this case). - **Rate-limit e2e in Chromium only**: all e2e requests come from one address, and the per-address login limit (20 in 15 minutes) would otherwise block the other browsers' runs. The per-address register limit (5 an hour) allows about one full suite run an hour.

- **Scrollbar gutter on touch devices**: not applied (touch screens overlay their scrollbars; in mobile WebKit the gutter made the page unsettled).
- **Fast profile shape**: the royale final ends at the first completed row (instead of the first full house) and every stage plays one round; the classic types are unchanged, so their rounds still end when every prize is won (about 55 draws for fullCard).
