## NEEDS THE OWNER'S WORD

- **Per-IP request rate at the proxy**: stock Caddy cannot limit requests per IP (a plugin and a custom Caddy build would be needed); the app now limits requests without a session to 600 a minute per address and guest sessions to 10 an hour, on top of the login and registration limits.
- **Your local development database** still holds the development accounts with their old fixed passwords (created before this fix); production never had them. Deleting them (the quick sign-in recreates them) would clear it.

- **One environment per server** (O5): staging beside prod would need a shared Caddy (standalone) or a second route such as `/games/bingo-staging` (website). The installer now refuses a second environment; staging runs on its own VPS until you want it built.
- **Log size** (O13): spec 08 §6 says 5 × 100 MB per service (up to about 1.5 GB for the three services, compressed); the compose file now follows the spec. Say if a smaller cap suits a small VPS better.
