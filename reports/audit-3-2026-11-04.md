Tests: no test deletes a match; e2e accounts are removed; no `.only`. Game: domain has no clock or randomness outside ports; tie-break, cut rounding, shield margin, quiet hours, D26–D33 as specified. Every source file has the standard header; no function over 60 lines; no TODO.
