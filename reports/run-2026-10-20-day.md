  domain's `evolve`, so the browser renders effects and never computes them.

  `components/SeasonIcon.tsx`, `components/CellFace.tsx`), the owner status
  panel, the cut screen, the classic prize list, `useReducedMotion.ts` and

  marks from the first draw on) and the emoji stand-ins for the season
  symbols. Reused: the domain's `evolve` and `createInitialMatchState`; the
  season colours in `types.ts`. New dev dependencies (web): `jsdom` 30.1.3
  (DOM for component tests), `@testing-library/react` 16.3.3 and

- **WP-07 gaps.** Eight WP-07 items have no test (table above). Proposal: a
  short follow-up branch `test/wp-07-gaps` with direct tests for round-end
  conditions, score carry, stage multiplier, skip stages and the jackpot guard.
- **Dead parallel classic implementation.** `classic/classicRound.ts`
  (`simulateClassicRound`) and `classic/classicPlacement.ts` are exported and
  tested but not called by the lifecycle. Proposal: delete them in that same
  follow-up, after moving their tests onto the lifecycle.
- **Manual-mode scoring in the browser.** In the dev-only Quick Test the
  browser scores the owner's own taps (`manualMarking.ts`), because the server
  pre-computes the whole match. CLAUDE.md says the client never computes
  scores; that rule is kept for the real game, where marking is validated by
  the server. The standings keep the server's auto-marked score for the owner,
  so taps never change bot results or cuts.
- **"Your cards: 1–4" are separate owner accounts.** The ruleset's
  `cardsPerPlayer` applies to every player, so the Quick Test gives the owner
  up to four accounts (`owner-01` … `owner-04`), each with its own card, score
  and cut outcome. Alternative: let one owner account hold several cards,
  which needs a per-player card count in the domain.
- **Slow property tests got a 30 s timeout.** `packages/domain/src/royale/
royale.properties.test.ts` › "simulated-match invariants" simulates up to 50
  full matches per property. It passes in under 3 s alone but crossed Vitest's
  5 s default once the suite grew, so the block now has an explicit 30 s budget.
