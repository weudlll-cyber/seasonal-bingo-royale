### What "correct" looks like per game type

- **royale.** You see a stage banner with `qualification / elimination /
final`. Each round ends on `drawCount` (default 40 for qualification), the
  cut screen says how many were eliminated and how many survived; a Winter
  shield save adds `(X shield save)`. If Season Complete and a full house
  fire for the same player in the same round, the jackpot banner shows the
  claimed value. The final stage ends on the first full house and the Final
  placement list shows every entrant (finalists ranked first, eliminated
  players by elimination order and then by score).
- **classicSeasons.** The 5×5 grid shows season icons at four cells (spring,
  summer, autumn, winter — colours from the brand palette). The draw ticker
  can hit a number or a symbol; drawing a symbol marks that symbol's cell on
  every card. Each `rows-N`, `columns-N`, `fourCorners`, `oneDiagonal`,
  `bothDiagonals`, `fullCard` prize ends up in the banner feed when the
  deciding draw lands; the round ends on the draw that awards the last
  outstanding prize.
- **classic.** As for `classicSeasons` but with 24 number cells (no symbols)
  and no Season Complete / jackpot (classic ignores those).

### How to play in manual mode

Change `Marking mode` to `manual` in the start form. Each drawn number ticks
the draw ticker; click (or focus and press Space / Enter) on the matching
cell on your card. A tap inside the speed window scores `pointsPerMark + the
speed bonus` and the feedback line shows `+10 pts (+10 speed bonus)`. A tap
on a number that has not been drawn is refused with `that number has not
been drawn — refused`. A drawn cell you miss auto-marks after a short wait
without the bonus and the feedback line says `auto-marked (window closed)`.

The owner's row in the standings is highlighted in Spring Green so you can
find yourself quickly even in a 200-player royale.
