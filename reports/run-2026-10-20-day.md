## FOR THE OWNER

**The game is running at http://localhost:5390.** Open it in your browser. The
small blue badge "Bingo · dev · 5390" in the corner shows it is the development
build.

### What to try

Fill in the form on the left and press **Run Quick Test**. Pick the game type,
type a seed (any word; the same seed always gives the same match), choose how
many bots play (3–199) and how many cards you get (1–4).

- **royale** — the battle royale: stages, cuts after each qualification round,
  season effects, Season Complete, jackpot, final.
- **classicSeasons** — one classic round; four season symbols are in the draw
  and on your card. Tick the prizes you want (five are ticked by default).
- **classic** — one classic 75-number round, numbers only.

One number is drawn at a time: **realtime** every 5 seconds (the ruleset draw
interval), **slower** every 10, **faster** every 1.25. **skip to end** jumps to
the result.

### How to play yourself (manual mode)

Set **Marking mode** to **manual (you tap)** and run with pace **realtime**.
When a number on your card is drawn, click it (or move to it with Tab and press
Enter or Space). Within 3 seconds of the draw you earn a speed bonus — the line
under your card says e.g. "Marked · +17 pts (speed bonus +7)". Clicking a number
that has not been drawn says "Not drawn yet — refused". If you miss a drawn
number, it is marked for you without bonus when its 3 seconds are over ("Auto-
marked … without bonus (window closed)").

### What "correct" looks like

- **royale:** the header shows the stage and round; after each cut a panel says
  "You survived" or "Eliminated – keep watching" (the match keeps playing to
  the end). Your panel shows points, rank, active effects with draws left
  (badges in the season colour), Winter shield charges and seasons collected.
  Standings show the top 20 plus your row. At a stage start points carry over
  as the ruleset says, so scores can drop to 0 at the final. The final
  placement lists finalists with points and eliminated players as "out".
- **classicSeasons / classic:** the prize list fills in as prizes are won; each
  prize goes only to the players who complete it on the draw that first
  completes it; the round ends on the draw that awards the last ticked prize.
  The final placement lists the prizes each player won (best prize first,
  then number of prizes, then marks). No season effects or Season Complete.
