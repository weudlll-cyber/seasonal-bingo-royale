  was red before the fix. Alternative: move them to their own branch first.
- **Remaining dead code.** `classic/classicRound.ts` (`simulateClassicRound`)
  is exported and tested but not called by the lifecycle; only its types are
  still used by `classicPlacement`. Proposal: remove it in the WP-07 follow-up.
- **Manual-mode scoring in the browser.** In the dev-only Quick Test the
  browser scores the owner's own taps (`manualMarking.ts`), because the server
  pre-computes the whole match. CLAUDE.md says the client never computes
  scores; that rule stays for the real game, where marking is validated by the
  server. The standings keep the server's auto-marked score for the owner, so
  taps never change bot results or cuts.
- **"Your cards: 1–4" are separate owner accounts** (`owner-01` … `owner-04`),
  each with its own card, score and cut outcome, because the ruleset's
  `cardsPerPlayer` applies to every player.
- **Classic rounds end on the deciding draw.** In the simulation every bot marks
  on the draw itself, so all simultaneous completers are in. In live realtime
  play with manual marking the round would end before slower players tap;
  that needs a rule (e.g. wait for the speed window) when realtime classic
  matches are built.
- **Slow property tests got a 30 s timeout** (`royale/royale.properties.test.ts`
  › "simulated-match invariants", `match/match.properties.test.ts` › both
  blocks); they simulate up to 50 full matches each, pass in a few seconds
  alone, but crossed Vitest's 5 s default under a busy parallel run.
