## FOR THE OWNER

**How to see it.** Sign in at http://localhost:5390 with Dev sign-in, choose Host a match, pick **Royale**; under **Between rounds** keep **Wait a few seconds** (15 by default). Create it, press **Fill with bots**, start it and draw through the first round. In the pause the console shows **Start next round now** and no Draw next; each player sees "Round over" in place of the last number. One click on **Start next round now** starts round 2 on every screen at once.
