## Screenshot review: reports/screens/real-game/

I opened all the `-view.jpg` files I list below, plus `-full.jpg` files for create-form (desktop and phone), royale-timed start/cut/end (host and phone), classicSeasons-timed mid (host and player-1) and classicSeasons-timed end player-1.

### Findings

1. **Season squares hide their number and mostly have no symbol** (classicSeasons-\*-player-\*, for example `classicSeasons-timed-mid-player-1-view.jpg` and `classicSeasons-timed-start-player-3-view.jpg`). Before they are marked, the season squares are plain yellow, blue or green blocks. Yellow and blue show nothing at all. Green shows a tiny dot you can barely see. Orange shows a faint ring. Nothing on the screen explains what these squares are or when they get marked. Once marked they show a proper icon (snowflake, sun, leaf, flower), so the empty unmarked look is inconsistent.

2. **Season balls in the drawn-numbers strip are unlabelled dots** (`classicSeasons-timed-mid-host-view.jpg`, `classicSeasons-host-end-player-2-view.jpg`). Every other chip reads like "G 52", but a season draw is just a coloured dot. The orange one is a ring while the others are solid. The last one in `classicSeasons-timed-end-player-3-view.jpg` is a square instead of a dot.

3. **The host's Standings cuts off player names** (`royale-timed-start/mid/cut-host-view.jpg`, `royale-timed-cut-host-full.jpg`). Names show as "dev-pla…", so dev-player-1/2/3 can't be told apart. In the "Out of the match" list the names shrink to "d…" and "b…". The phone view does the same, including the player's own highlighted row ("de…") in `royale-host-end-player-3-view.jpg` and `royale-timed-cut-player-3-full.jpg`. Player desktop views show full names in a panel of the same width.

4. **The Standings panel is not the same width on every screen.** In `royale-host-start-host-view.jpg` and `royale-host-mid-host-view.jpg` it reaches about x=1205. On every other screen it stops at about x=1183, in line with the page column.

5. **Timed matches show a large greyed-out "Draw next" button** (`*-timed-*-host-view.jpg`). It is the most prominent control on the host console but does nothing in timed mode. The only hint is a small "Timed draws every 4 seconds." line.

6. **The host is not told about a cut** (`royale-timed-cut-host-view.jpg`, `royale-host-cut-host-view.jpg`). The top of the screen just says "Waiting for the first number" or shows the next ball. The cut summary ("Cut 1: 13 go on, 7 are out…") only appears in the Match log at the bottom of the full page. The host also has no LIVE badge and no stage/round/draw line, while players see "stage 1 (qualification) · round 2 · draw 0".

7. **An eliminated player gets the same message three times** (`royale-timed-cut-player-3-view.jpg`, `royale-host-cut-player-1-view.jpg`). They see "You're out in cut 1 — you can keep watching" with a "Keep watching" button, then "You were eliminated – keep watching as a spectator.", then "Out in cut 1". It is not clear what the button does since they are already watching. Likewise, a player who survives sees a "Back to the game" button (`royale-timed-cut-player-1/2-view.jpg`) while the game is already right below it.

8. **End screens still use in-play wording.** "You were eliminated – keep watching as a spectator" and "Speed window: 3 s after each draw" stay after the match is finished (`royale-timed-end-player-3-view.jpg`, `royale-host-end-player-3-view.jpg`). The ball says "Round over" instead of "Match over".

9. **End screens don't say what to do next** (all `*-end-*`). There is no "Back to lobby", "Play again" or "Host another match" button for either the host or the players.

10. **The royale final placement looks wrong without an explanation** (`royale-timed-end-host-view.jpg`). The winner has 990 pts, #2 and #3 have 230, and #6 (out) has 1019 pts. Placement goes by survival, not points, but nothing says so. In classic, bots tied at 240 pts get ranks #1–#17 with no tie shown (`classicSeasons-timed-end-player-1-view.jpg`). The classic podium shows #2 and #3 as "no prize" with no points (`classic-host-end-host-view.jpg`), so the reason for their order isn't visible.

11. **The ranking appears twice on end screens** (`royale-timed-end-host-full.jpg`). "Final placement" and "Standings" list the same 20 rows one after the other.

12. **Technical terms are visible.**
    - The match title "Acceptance classicSeasons-timed" is camelCase. It is test data, but it shows in the title.
    - The nav bar shows "Dev sign-in" next to "Sign out" even when someone is signed in, on every screen.
    - Lobby and host-list titles look like "Nav 1792597950570" and "Smoke 1792…" (`lobby-*`, `create-form-*-full.jpg`).
    - The Prizes panel shows a lowercase status word, "open".
    - The match log says "1 are out" (`royale-timed-end-host-full.jpg`).

13. **The create form is not consistent** (`create-form-desktop-view.jpg`).
    - The Draws and Missed numbers sections use unstyled browser dropdowns and small grey inputs, unlike the styled fields above them.
    - The dropdown text repeats its heading ("Draws: timed…" under "Draws").
    - The "Seconds between timed draws" box is greyed and hard to read.
    - In `create-form-desktop-full.jpg`, the "dbg" rows put Manage on the right while every other row puts it below.

14. **On the phone, the create form is hard to reach and the header is crowded** (`create-form-phone-view.jpg`, `create-form-phone-full.jpg`). About 25 "Your matches" rows (many old "Running" test matches) push the form to the very bottom of a 4,149-px page. The header wraps to three rows and takes about 200 px on every phone screen.

15. **The lobby shows stopped matches** (`lobby-desktop-view.jpg`). "Stopped" matches with a "View" button appear in a list described as "Join an open match, or watch one that is running."

16. **Phone match titles wrap at the hyphen** ("Acceptance royale- / timed") in `royale-timed-start-player-3-full.jpg`.

17. **The shield pill has blank space or a hidden icon** (`royale-*-player-*`). "shield ×0" has empty space on its left, which looks like a blue icon on a blue background.

18. **The "Marked" label under the card is unclear** (most player views). It is static text and doesn't say what was marked. On the cut screen it becomes "Tap a drawn number on your card.", which is the useful version.

### Phone layout

It works overall. There is no horizontal overflow, and the 5×5 card fits at a good size. The weak points are the three-row header (item 14), the card and the ball not fitting on screen together, and the truncated names (item 3).

### What works well

- The ball, the drawn-numbers chips and the speed bar are clear and the same everywhere.
- The card is very readable, and marked cells (dark with an orange border) are obvious.
- Rules are in plain English ("Draws: host presses Draw next · Missed numbers: left open (tap later without bonus)").
- The waiting room is clean: "You are in. The host starts the match."
- The Prizes panel with winner, draw and number is clear, and the match-log lines read naturally.
- "You finished #12 of 20" plus the podium is a strong end summary.
- The "Out of the match" section with "out" tags makes the royale results easy to scan.
- The own row in Standings is highlighted consistently.
- No error codes or raw keys appear anywhere in the game screens.
