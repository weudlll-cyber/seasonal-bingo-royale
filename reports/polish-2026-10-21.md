## FOR THE OWNER

**How to try it**

1. Open http://localhost:5390 in a normal window. Choose **Dev sign-in → Host**. The chip at the top says "Signed in as dev-host (host)".
2. **Host a match**: fill in a title, pick a game type and draws, and press Create. You land on the host console.
3. Open a **private window** (or another browser), go to http://localhost:5390 and choose **Dev sign-in → Player 1**. One window holds one sign-in, so the second person needs a private window. In the lobby, press **Join** on your match: you are in the waiting room.
4. Back in the host window, press **Play** in the top bar. Your match shows **Manage** instead of Join, and every card shows its code. **Manage** brings you back to the console. The "Host a match" page also lists **Your matches**.
5. Fill with bots, press **Start match**, and play. From the player screen the host can always return with **Open host console**.

**What changed on screen**

- Every message is plain English: "Round 1 over — all 40 numbers drawn", "Cut 1: 12 go on, 8 are out (2 saved by Winter shield)", "Full card won by bot-02 on draw 37 (G 52)". No internal words or error codes.
- After a cut: "You're through — next round in 6 s" or "You're out in cut 1 — you can keep watching". The message comes into view even if you scrolled down to your card. An eliminated player's panel says "Out in cut 1".
- One match log, newest first.
- Standings are ranked rows with the four season icons. Your row is highlighted, and players who are out are dimmed below a divider.
- At the end: "You finished #9 of 20", a podium for the top three, the full list with one line on how places are decided, and buttons "Back to the lobby" / "Host another match".
- Host console: the same progress line and LIVE badge as the players. "Host draws / Timed draws" is a switch; Draw next appears only for host draws, and the seconds field only for timed draws.
- Classic with seasons: season squares show their icon on the card, and a drawn season reads "Winter" with its icon.
- Phone: nothing is wider than the screen, and the top bar scrolls away.
- Screenshots to look at: `reports/screens/real-game/` (lobby, create form, waiting room at desktop and phone size; the first draw of a battle royale for the host and all three players).

### Phone layout

It works overall. There is no horizontal overflow, and the 5×5 card fits at a good size. The weak points are the three-row header (item 14), the card and the ball not fitting on screen together, and the truncated names (item 3).

### What works well

- The ball, the drawn-numbers chips and the speed bar are clear and the same everywhere.
- The card is very readable, and marked cells (dark with an orange border) are obvious.
- Rules are in plain English ("Draws: host presses Draw next · Missed numbers: left open (tap later without bonus)").
- The waiting room is clean: "You are in. The host starts the match."
- The Prizes panel with winner, draw and number is clear, and the match-log lines read naturally.
- "You finished #12 of 20" plus the podium is a strong end summary.
- The "Out of the match" section with "out" tags makes the royale results easy to scan.
- The own row in Standings is highlighted consistently.
- No error codes or raw keys appear anywhere in the game screens.
